## 2. Included Capabilities in the $0.00 Edition

- **Dual-Mode Automatic & Manual Zoom:** Follow Cursor auto-pan and Fixed Spot target-dot zoom (`1.25x–2.80x` presets + custom multiplier) with Perlin 5th-order polynomial smootherstep easing, 3 camera feel profiles (`Smooth`, `Focused`, `Tight`), and a 36px camera deadzone.
- **Smooth Cursor Trajectory & Click FX:** Catmull-Rom spline cursor interpolation, cursor highlight halo, and 4 click-pulse animations (`Radiating Ripple`, `Ripple + Glow Orb`, `Expanding Shockwave`, `Classic Focal Orb`) across 1–3 harmonic waves.
- **Zoom-Synced & Screen-Locked Privacy Blur:** Draw interactive blur boxes directly on the video canvas with 3 redaction styles (`Frosted Glass Blur`, `Pixelate / Mosaic`, `Redacted Blackout`), configurable intensity (`4px–48px`), and timed timeline segments.
- **Draggable 1:1 Studio Webcam Overlay:** Circular or rounded-square Picture-in-Picture camera bubble with `Fixed Position`, `Smart Dodge (Avoid Cursor)`, and `Zoom With Screen` modes.
- **Full Non-Linear Demo Editor:** Scissor trim handles (`I`/`O`), playhead split (`S`), Clean Browser tab cropping, macOS window frame header, 24 backdrops (12 macOS wallpapers + 12 gradients + custom upload), and 9 aspect ratios (`Native`, `16:9`, `9:16`, `1:1`, `4:5`, `4:3`, `16:10`, `3:2`, `21:9`).
- **100% Local Privacy Architecture:** All recordings are stored in local browser `IndexedDB` (`CursorDemoVideoStorage`) and encoded on-device.
