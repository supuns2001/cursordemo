What is a screen recorder with automatic zoom?

CursorDemo is a free, open-source Chrome screen recorder with automatic zoom that tracks cursor movements and mouse clicks to generate cinematic product demos. It combines real-time Catmull-Rom cursor smoothing, Perlin smootherstep camera easing, interactive privacy blur, and local 4K 60fps MP4 export with zero watermarks or cloud uploads.

In modern SaaS product marketing and technical documentation, viewers watching a standard 1080p or 4K screencast on mobile or laptop viewports often struggle to read dense UI controls. CursorDemo solves this problem entirely inside the browser via a dual-stream Manifest V3 architecture. During capture, a lightweight content script samples DOM pointer coordinates, click events, and scroll deltas at 16ms intervals while MediaRecorder captures high-bitrate video into client-side IndexedDB storage. When recording finishes, the built-in non-linear editor analyzes click clusters and cursor dwell velocity to synthesize automatic zoom segments. Camera pan trajectories are interpolated using Catmull-Rom splines paired with Ken Perlin’s 5th-order polynomial smootherstep curve and a 36-pixel deadzone threshold that eliminates micro-jitter during typing or reading. Unlike paid desktop recorders costing $89 to $229 per seat or cloud tools that watermark free exports, CursorDemo renders 4K 60fps MP4 and WebM video locally on macOS, Windows, Linux, and ChromeOS under the permissive MIT software license.

How does CursorDemo track your cursor and zoom the camera?

CursorDemo tracks your mouse at 60 frames per second while you record your screen. When you stop, the editor finds your clicks and pauses on the page. It then builds smooth Perlin curve zoom blocks with a 36-pixel deadzone so the view glides without shake.

Key Recording and Studio Editing Features

  • Dual Zoom Modes: Use Follow Cursor mode to glide with your mouse. Or pick Fixed Spot mode to lock onto a form with a purple dot.
  • Smooth Cursor & Click Ripples: Turn shaky hand paths into smooth curves. Add a soft cursor halo and show clean pulse rings on each click.
  • Zoom-Synced Privacy Blur: Draw glass, pixel, or dark boxes on the canvas. They can track zoomed UI items or stay pinned in place.
  • Smart-Dodge Face Cam: Show a 1:1 webcam bubble on stage. It can stay fixed, zoom with the screen, or dodge your mouse.
  • Studio Frames & 9 Aspect Ratios: Crop browser tabs and add macOS window frames. Pick from 24 backdrops across 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3, 16:10, 3:2, or 21:9 sizes.
  • Local 4K 60fps MP4 Export: Save fast H.264 MP4 or VP9 WebM files on your disk. There are no signups, no cloud servers, and no watermarks.

Five Simple Steps to Record a Product Demo

  1. Add CursorDemo from the Chrome Web Store or load the folder in Chrome.
  2. Click the toolbar icon to open the studio. Pick your mic, system sound, and face cam.
  3. Choose the tab, window, or screen you want to show. Walk through your app at your normal pace.
  4. Click Stop to open the studio editor. Your auto zoom blocks and click ripples are ready on the timeline.
  5. Pick a backdrop and draw blur boxes over private text. Export a 1080p or 4K 60fps MP4 file to your drive.

Frequently Asked Questions About CursorDemo

Is CursorDemo really 100% free with no watermarks or time limits?

Yes, CursorDemo is 100% free under the open-source MIT license. There are no paid plans, no watermarks on your videos, and no time caps beyond your own free disk space.

Which browsers and operating systems does CursorDemo support?

CursorDemo works seamlessly on any Chromium-based browser—including Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Opera / Opera GX, Yandex Browser, Vivaldi, and Arc—across Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux, and ChromeOS.

Does CursorDemo send my videos or mouse data to any cloud server?

No, CursorDemo never sends your video, sound, or mouse path to any server. Each clip is saved inside your browser’s local IndexedDB store (CursorDemoVideoStorage) and rendered right on your device.

Can I edit, split, or remove the auto zoom blocks after recording?

Yes, every zoom block shows up on the timeline in the studio editor. You can drag trim handles, split blocks at the playhead, switch between Follow and Fixed Spot modes, or delete any block.

What video formats, sizes, and aspect ratios can I export?

CursorDemo exports fast H.264 MP4 and VP9 WebM files at 60 frames per second. You can export in 720p, 1080p, 2K, or 4K across 9 aspect ratios: Native, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3, 16:10, 3:2, and 21:9.

Why don’t auto zoom and click ripples run automatically outside a browser tab?

Browser security sandboxes only let content scripts track live mouse coordinates inside standard web pages. When you record an app outside the browser or an internal settings page, video and sound still record cleanly, and you can add Follow or Fixed Spot zoom blocks and blur masks in the editor.

How many languages does CursorDemo support on the Chrome Web Store?

CursorDemo ships with support for 56 Chrome Web Store locales so teams around the world can find and use the tool in their own language.
